# Product

## Register

Brand. This is the marketing landing page for Iveo. Design IS the product — the site itself is the sales pitch and proof of capability.

## Users & Purpose

**Primary users:** Norwegian small and medium-sized businesses (SMB) and solo entrepreneurs who need a modern website but don't have technical resources. Secondary: dentists, gyms, restaurants, consultants, local services.

**Their context:** Researching website providers, often comparing 2-3 options. Skeptical of agencies that overcharge, but want quality and reliability. Mobile-first (most arrive from phone).

**Job to be done:** "I need a website that makes my business look professional, loads fast, and I don't have to think about. I want one provider I can trust to handle everything."

**Emotions to evoke:** Confidence (this team knows what they're doing), trust (real Norwegian people behind it), excitement (the future, AI, modern tech), simplicity (no jargon, easy to understand).

## Brand & Personality

**Three words:** Futuristic, warm, capable.

The tension is intentional — Iveo is bleeding-edge tech (Next.js, AI) delivered by a real father-and-son team. The site should feel like talking to a competent friend who happens to build with the same tools as Netflix and TikTok.

**Brand pillars:**
- **Speed** — delivery within one week, fast websites, fast support
- **Personal** — father-son team, not a faceless agency
- **Future-ready** — AI products coming, modern stack, built for what's next

**Reference brands (the right feel):**
- **Linear** — clean, technical, confident, restrained color
- **Vercel** — minimal, gradient accents used sparingly, code-confident
- **Resend** — friendly tech, glassy depth used purposefully

**Anti-references (what NOT to look like):**
- Generic agency template ("we are passionate about your success")
- Crypto/web3 aesthetic (heavy neon, animated stars, "revolutionary")
- 2018 SaaS landing page (hero + 3 feature cards + pricing tiers + CTA)
- Corporate stock-photo websites (smiling team in conference room)
- Wix/Squarespace default look (predictable, identical to 10000 other sites)

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA target — all body text must hit 4.5:1 contrast against background
- Reduced-motion support — every animation needs a static fallback
- Norwegian as primary language; English friendly for international clarity
- Mobile-first responsive — most visitors arrive from phone
- Keyboard navigation for all interactive elements (CTAs, chatbot, nav)

## Strategic principles

1. **Show, don't tell.** The website itself is the proof of what we can build. If a visitor doubts our skill, this site has failed.
2. **Talk like a human.** No "leverage", "empower", "transformative". Plain Norwegian, occasional warmth.
3. **One clear next step.** Every section should make booking a meeting or messaging the chatbot feel natural.
4. **Speed matters everywhere.** Page must load fast, chatbot responds in seconds, real follow-up within hours.
5. **No fake stats or testimonials.** Trust is earned. Don't fabricate proof we haven't earned yet.
